# E-Commerce Consumer & Purchasing Behaviour Analytics

### From Click to Return: Understanding the Customer Journey

## 1. Project Overview

This project explores e-commerce consumer behaviour through the complete customer journey, from purchasing decisions to post-purchase satisfaction and product returns.

Using a synthetic e-commerce dataset sourced from Kaggle, the project examines customer demographics, purchasing habits, sales performance, and customer experience.

**Business objectives:**
- Identify customer segments and purchasing patterns.
- Examine shopping channels, payment preferences, and purchasing behaviour.
- Investigate customer satisfaction and product return patterns.
- Generate actionable insights to support marketing strategies, customer experience, and operational improvements.

Two interactive Power BI dashboards were developed for **Sales & Marketing** and **Customer Service** analysis.

## 2. Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, preprocessing, missing-value handling, and data preparation.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI monitoring, data visualisation, and exploratory analysis.
- **Data Source:** Synthetic e-commerce customer dataset from Kaggle (12,370 original rows and 30 attributes).

## 3. Methodology

**Data Cleaning & Preparation**

- Identified and handled missing values and data inconsistencies.
- Standardised data formats to support consistent analysis.
- Derived analytical variables, including Purchase Bucket, Age Group, and Decision Speed.

**Exploratory Data Analysis**

- Examined demographic and socio-economic profiles, including age, occupation, income, and location.
- Analysed purchasing channels, payment methods, shopping times, and sales patterns.
- Explored relationships among customer satisfaction, purchasing behaviour, product ratings, and returns.

**Dashboard Development**

- **Sales & Marketing Dashboard:** Visualised customer demographics, sales trends, purchasing locations, payment preferences, and device usage.
- **Customer Service Dashboard:** Examined return rates, satisfaction scores, purchasing channels, decision speed, discount sensitivity, and shipping preferences.

## 4. Key Findings

| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| Product Return Rate | **49.79%** |
| Online Purchase Share | **64.82%** |
| Average Customer Satisfaction | **5.54** |
| Average Purchase Decision Time | **14.99 days** |
| Leading Purchase Location | **Denver** |
| Most Common Purchase Period | **Morning** |

**Analytical Insights**

- **High product returns:** Nearly half of purchases were recorded as returns, highlighting a potential concern for fulfilment costs, profitability, and customer retention.
- **Online purchasing dominance:** Online transactions accounted for 64.82% of purchases, demonstrating the importance of digital shopping channels.
- **Lengthy purchasing decisions:** Customers took approximately 15 days to make purchasing decisions, suggesting opportunities to investigate purchasing hesitation and product information needs.
- **Customer experience:** The average satisfaction score of 5.54 indicates potential opportunities for improvement, subject to the dataset's rating scale.

## 5. Business Recommendations

**Improve Product Information**

Provide clearer product descriptions, specifications, and images to reduce uncertainty and potential mismatches between customer expectations and purchases.

**Optimise Digital Shopping Experience**

Prioritise mobile-friendly navigation, accessible product information, and a convenient checkout experience to support digital customers.

**Enhance Promotional Strategies**

Explore targeted promotions during popular purchasing periods and test approaches that support customers with longer decision times.

**Strengthen Returns Management**

Investigate return reasons by product category, purchasing channel, and customer satisfaction level to identify potential areas for improvement.

**Improve Customer Support**

Strengthen pre-purchase guidance and post-purchase assistance to address customer concerns and potentially improve satisfaction.

*These recommendations are based on exploratory findings. Further investigation is required to establish the causes of returns and evaluate the effectiveness of proposed improvements.*
